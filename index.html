About LIRA OS

LIRA OS is a modular artificial intelligence assistant ecosystem designed to support creative projects, organization, communication and business workflows.

LIRA MAIN serves as the central coordinator of specialized AI assistants.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

LIRA OS is under development. This policy describes its intended handling of data through authorized integrations.

Depending on the features enabled, LIRA OS may process messages, contact identifiers, communication metadata and information voluntarily submitted by users.

Such information is intended to be used to provide requested assistant functions, process communications and maintain technical reliability.

Authorized service providers, including hosting and messaging platforms, may process information necessary to operate these integrations.

LIRA OS does not intend to sell personal information. Appropriate access controls and security measures will be maintained.

Users may request information about their personal data, correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

This policy will be updated as the service and its integrations develop.

User Data Deletion Instructions

Users may request deletion of personal information processed by LIRA OS.

To request deletion, contact the project administrator using the official contact channel provided for the service.

Please include sufficient information to identify the relevant account or communication, without sending passwords or other sensitive credentials.

Requests will be reviewed and processed subject to identity verification, applicable law and legitimate retention obligations.
